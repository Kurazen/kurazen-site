Kurazen

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 July 2026

In short: your collection stays on your device. Kurazen has no accounts and no servers that store your data. We do not collect personal information.

1. We don't collect personal information

Kurazen has no account and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and there is no server that stores your data. Everything you add — your collection, prices, notes, favourites, and settings — is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device.

2. Your collection stays on your device

Your catalogue never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it. The app's CSV export creates a file that you control and share yourself, for backup or moving to a new phone. We never receive a copy.

3. Information sent to third parties (only to look things up)

To fetch cover art, titles, release dates, scores and similar details, the app sends the search terms and barcodes you enter to these public databases:

  • IGDB / Twitch — game data — privacy notice
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and TV data — privacy policy
  • UPCitemdb — barcode lookups — website

These requests contain only what is needed for the lookup (for example, a title you typed or a barcode you scanned). They do not include your identity, and no personal profile is built. Lookups happen only when you take an action, and results are cached on your device so the same thing is not fetched twice.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Game data is powered by IGDB.

4. Camera

The app uses your camera only while the barcode scanner is open, to read a barcode on screen. Photos are not taken, stored, or uploaded. You can decline the camera permission and add items by title instead.

5. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no behavioural tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

Some screens offer optional links to external stores and reference sites (for example eBay, Amazon, or PriceCharting) so you can look a title up or buy it. Tapping one opens your browser. If commission (affiliate) links are enabled in a future version, that will be disclosed in the app, and it still involves no sharing of personal data.

6. Children

Kurazen is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your control

Because your data lives on your device, you control it:

  • Delete any item, or delete everything, at any time inside the app.
  • Export a copy for yourself at any time via CSV.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version published at this address.
